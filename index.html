Return & Exchange Policy

Your satisfaction is our priority. We offer a hassle-free 7-day replacement policy.

7-Day Replacement Guarantee

If you receive a damaged, defective, or wrong item, you can request a replacement within 7 days of delivery.

Conditions for Return/Exchange:

  • The product must be unused, unwashed, and in its original condition.
  • Original tags, packaging, and seals must be intact.
  • A clear video of the unboxing is highly recommended for damaged item claims.

Non-Returnable Items:

For hygiene reasons, opened makeup products, earrings, and personalized items cannot be returned or exchanged unless they are factory defective.
